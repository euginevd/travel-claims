undercoverTravel insurance

Working pilot

AI agents decide the routine claims. Claims experts decide the rest.

UnderCover is an end-to-end AI agents system for travel insurance claims. Customers claim online, each AI decision cites its sources, and a person signs off the larger payouts. Every decision is traced end to end and tested against unseen claims and attack claims.
