undercoverTravel insurance

Working prototype

AI agents decide the routine claims. Claims experts decide the rest.

UnderCover is an AI agents system for travel insurance claims, built end to end on AWS. Customers claim online, every AI decision cites its sources, and a person signs off the larger payouts. Each decision is traced, scored against claims the agent has never seen, and tested with attack claims.
