# Code of conduct

**Be kind, be useful, and assume good intent. This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).**

[Our pledge](#our-pledge) · [Expected](#expected) · [Not accepted](#not-accepted) · [Reporting](#reporting) · [Enforcement](#enforcement)

</div>

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected

1. Respect differing opinions, experience and ways of working.
2. Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
3. Own your mistakes, apologise to anyone they affected, and learn from them.
4. Put what's best for the people using the project ahead of winning an argument.

## Not accepted

1. Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
2. Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
3. Harassment, in public or in private.
4. Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
5. Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a breach privately to the maintainer: the repo's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability** reaches them without a public trace, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes. Every report is read, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban someone, for a time or for good, after a warning or straight away for a serious breach. It applies in the project's spaces and wherever someone represents the project.
