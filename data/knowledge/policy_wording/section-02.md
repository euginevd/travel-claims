# 2. Trip Cancellation and Interruption

We pay when you have to cancel your trip before it starts, or cut it short, for one of these reasons:

1. Sickness, injury or death of you, a traveling companion or a family member, confirmed in writing by a physician.
2. Severe weather or a natural disaster that stops your carrier's service for 24 hours or more.
3. Your home or your destination is made uninhabitable by fire, flood or storm.
4. You are called for jury duty or served with a court subpoena.
5. You lose your job through no fault of your own after at least one year with the employer.

We do not pay for cancellation or interruption for any other reason.

For a cancellation we pay the prepaid trip costs that your suppliers will not refund. For an interruption we pay the unused prepaid costs that will not be refunded, plus a one-way economy fare home. We pay up to the trip cost on your policy record or your plan's maximum, whichever is lower. Refunds and credits from suppliers are taken off first.
