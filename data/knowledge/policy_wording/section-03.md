# 3. Trip Delay

We pay when your carrier delays you for at least your plan's waiting period because of a mechanical or crew problem, a canceled service, severe weather or a strike. The delay runs from the scheduled departure time to the time you actually depart.

We pay reasonable extra costs for meals, accommodation, local transport and rebooking fees during the delay, up to your plan's maximum. Send us the receipts and the carrier's confirmation of the delay and its cause. If a delay on your way home keeps you traveling past your return date, your cover continues until you get home.
